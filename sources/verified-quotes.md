## NBTC India (MoHFW / NACO 2017 guidelines, via MoHFW summary + PMC6825231)
- "The age group should be 18-65 years."
- "Weight at least 45 Kg."
- "Should be more than or equal to 12.5g/dL." (haemoglobin)
- "For whole blood donation, once in three months (90 days) for males and four months (120 days) for females."
- "Apheresis should be done only after 90 days of whole blood collection or in an event when red cells are not returned at the end of pheresis."

## American Red Cross (redcrossblood.org eligibility + platelet pages)
- "You must wait at least 8 weeks (56 days) between donations of whole blood."
- "Platelet donors can donate once every 7 days, up to 24 times a year."
- "You must wait at least 28 days between plasma donations, up to 13 times/year."
- "You can donate whole blood every 56 days, up to 6 times a year."
- "You must be at least 17 years old in most states (16 years old with parental consent in some states)."
- "You must weigh at least 110 lbs."

## UK NHSBT (blood.co.uk 'Who can give blood')
- "Men can give blood every 12 weeks and women can give blood every 16 weeks."
- "be aged 17 to 65 if it's your first donation, or up to 72 if you've donated before"
- "weigh between 7 stone 12 lbs (50kg) and 25 stone (158kg)"

## Blood-group matrix
Derived from ABO/Rh antigen–antibody rules; cross-checked against the AABB
Technical Manual and redcrossblood.org/donate-blood/blood-types.html.
Spot facts confirmed: O- is the universal red-cell donor (gives to all 8);
AB+ is the universal red-cell recipient (receives from all 8); AB (AB+ and AB-)
is the universal plasma donor (plasma compatible with all 8).
